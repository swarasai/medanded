Book Drive

Med n’ Ed has partnered up with Healthy Habits for Children to bring exposure to books made for kids. Victoria (author and founder of Healthy Habits for Children) has written many books to educate children and has given them to Med n’ Ed for us to share them around the world. We have given these books to Smile Canada, Big Brothers Big Sisters, Friendship Circle, Children’s Home Society of California, YMCA Canada, Action for Children, The Honeypot Children’s Charity, The Canadian Children’s Book Centre, JaneFinch Reaching Up, and First Book Canada. These organizations that we have given the books to are spreading them to children in their area and helping them learn!

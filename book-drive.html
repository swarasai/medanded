Book Drive

Med n’ Ed partnered with Healthy Habits for Children to bring exposure to books made for kids. Victoria, the author and founder, created books designed to educate children and shared them with Med n’ Ed so they could be distributed around the world.

Books were shared with organizations including Smile Canada, Big Brothers Big Sisters, Friendship Circle, Children’s Home Society of California, YMCA Canada, Action for Children, The Honeypot Children’s Charity, The Canadian Children’s Book Centre, JaneFinch Reaching Up, and First Book Canada.

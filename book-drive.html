Book Drive

Med n’ Ed has partnered up with Healthy Habits for Children to bring exposure to books made for kids. Victoria, the author and founder of Healthy Habits for Children, has written books to educate children and has given them to Med n’ Ed for us to share around the world.

We have given these books to organizations including Smile Canada, Big Brothers Big Sisters, Friendship Circle, Children’s Home Society of California, YMCA Canada, Action for Children, The Honeypot Children’s Charity, The Canadian Children’s Book Centre, JaneFinch Reaching Up, and First Book Canada.

These organizations are spreading the books to children in their area and helping them learn!

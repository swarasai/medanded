Doctors’ Aspects on Healthcare

Med n’ Ed has hosted interview series featuring medical professionals and students discussing healthcare, medicine, education, and their career journeys.

Episode #1 — Dr. Ihssan Kaadan

Insights on healthcare, doctors, and the pandemic, including the importance of protecting yourself and others.

Doctor Hardeep Kataria

An interview about healthcare, optometry, education, empowerment, fitness, and medicine.

Episode #11 — Dr. Srushti Sahukar

A discussion of medical electives, away rotations, and medical education experiences.

The Doctor’s Journey — Farah Dehzad

A conversation about medical school, pre-med preparation, and the path toward becoming a physician.

Dr. Alex Schupper

A neurosurgery resident discusses the journey to medicine and becoming a subspecialist.

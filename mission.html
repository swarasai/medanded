About Us

Our Mission

Med n’ Ed is a nonprofit organization committed to giving proper medical and educational access to all in need. We spread awareness to youth about hygiene, sanity, nutrition, water safety while supporting the underprivileged in healthcare aspects. We gather a team of professionals that first hand gives treatments to underprivileged people along with providing them supplies. Locally, our work involves donations, fundraisers, and hands on projects/events to help the uderpriveledged in medical and educational aspects. It is our duty to give everyone the healthcare that they deserve.

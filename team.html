Our Board

Sadhika Mulagari

Founder and President

Sadhika is a sophomore at UCLA. She founded Med n’ Ed because she realized that, not many people have the same privilege as she is fortunate enough to have, especially in healthcare aspects. She feels that health is one place, nobody should compromise on but are forced to based on various factors. Some fun facts about her is that she loves to dance, fence, and paint. Sadhika has also participated in several other service-related organizations such as Saving Hearts Foundation, SAF Coordinator for Red Cross, outreach coordinator of AMSA, and leader/teacher at InFuse. Sadhika knows that together we can medicate and educate all in need!

Vice-President

In her free time, Roshni loves art, badminton, spending time with her family, and dance. She loves to involve herself in the community and has participated in Speech & Debate, Interact, BPA, Badminton, Voiceopedia, and WhizFin.

Sudhiksha Das

Secretary

Sudiksha Das is a board member of Med n’ Ed as well as a sophomore at Georgia Tech. Her main reason for being in Med n’ Ed is her desire to be part of an organization that highlights student voices and allows young individuals to come together and make a change. She is passionate about the many projects Med n’ Ed partakes in and believes that with hard work, this organization will be able to help medicate and educate those in need.

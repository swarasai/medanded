Becoming a Medical Professional, Webinar Series
This is a partnership with Teens for Healthcare. In this 4 webinar series we bring in special guests, premeds, medical students, students in their residency, and lastly a professional. Each talk about their experiences, how they got there and how you can too. The purpose of this series was to allow people to understand better what exactly the steps are and what you need to become a healthcare professional. Following along with this series definitely gave our audience a much better understanding!

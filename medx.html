MedX Conference

On May 1, 2021, Med n’ Ed partnered with The Ripple Effect and Med Debrief to initiate, develop, and host an online conference known as MedX. MedX encourages youth to educate themselves about humanitarian issues from a medical standpoint. The conference focused on healthcare for refugees and the general refugee crisis.

Guest speakers Doctor Zahra Goliaei and medical student Sumara Stroshein discussed the refugee crisis from a global perspective and the importance of becoming involved with humanitarian organizations. Med n’ Ed, Med Debrief, and The Ripple Effect representatives discussed medical issues commonly faced by refugees, possible solutions, ways to take action, and useful resources. The conference ended with an online escape-room simulation designed to deepen participants’ understanding of the refugee crisis and common obstacles refugees face.

Med n’ Ed hopes to continue MedX as a series discussing lesser-known issues from a medical perspective and educating the public.

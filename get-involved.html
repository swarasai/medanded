Want to Get Involved?

Thank you for showing interest in involving yourselves in our organization! We could have never gotten to where we are today without our volunteers and our wonderful members. We are extremely passionate about making a difference in any way possible, and we would like for you to join us and make a difference too!

There are many ways for you to help. You can become a member and assist us to work towards this cause. Partnering with us is another great way to help, giving us a chance to expand and reach people throughout the world. Lastly, donations help us aid people who are in need and fund our ongoing projects.

Become a Member

Med n’ Ed is a student-led nonprofit organization with over 100+ members or volunteers. Members can help the cause, earn volunteer hours, explore departments, find opportunities, and gain leadership experience.

Partner With Us

Med n’ Ed works with organizations, companies, hospitals, laboratories, and others. Partnerships help expand reach, work with others, and support a wider cause.

Donate

Donations support ongoing projects and help us medicate and educate people in need. Every dollar counts.

Want to Get Involved?

Thank you for showing interest in involving yourselves in our organization! We could have never gotten to where we are today without our volunteers and our wonderful members. We are extremely passionate about making a difference in any way possible, and we would like for you to join us and make a difference too!

There are many ways for you to help. You can become a member and assist us to work towards this cause. Partnering with us is another great way to help, we get a chance to expand and reach out to other people throughout the world. Lastly, donations help us aid people who are in need and fund the ongoing projects we have. As Earl Nightingale once said, “Don’t wait for change, be the change.” So let’s change the world one step at a time. Together.

Become A Member

Med n’ Ed is a student-led nonprofit organization of over 100+ members or volunteers. By becoming a member of this Med n’ Ed you will have yourself a great opportunity to help this wonderful cause, earn many volunteer hours, explore departments, find yourself opportunities and have exemplary leadership experience!

Partner With Us

Med n’ Ed does many events with other organizations, companies, hospitals, laboratories, etc. If you would like us to partner, support, work with, or fund you, please fill out the link form. Partnering with us allows you to expand your scope, work with others, and help a wider cause!

Donate

Med n’ Ed has many ongoing projects which requires your help! Please deeply consider donating now in order to medicate and educate all in need. All of your proceeds go purely to the cause we work for and help those who cannot help themselves. Every dollar counts!

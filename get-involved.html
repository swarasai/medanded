Join Us

Want to Get Involved?

Thank you for showing interest in involving yourselves in our organization! We could have never gotten to where we are today without our volunteers and our wonderful members. We are extremely passionate about making a difference in any way possible, and we would like for you to join us and make a difference too!

There are many ways for you to help. You can become a member and assist us to work towards this cause. Partnering with us is another great way to help, we get a chance to expand and reach out to other people throughout the world. Lastly, donations help us aid people who are in need and fund the ongoing projects we have. As Earl Nightingale once said, “Don’t wait for change, be the change.” So let’s change the world one step at a time. Together.

01

Become a Member

Med n’ Ed is a student-led nonprofit organization of over 100+ members or volunteers. By becoming a member you will have an opportunity to help this cause, earn volunteer hours, explore departments, find opportunities, and gain leadership experience.

02

Partner With Us

Med n’ Ed does many events with other organizations, companies, hospitals, laboratories, and others. Partnering with us allows you to expand your scope, work with others, and help a wider cause.

03

Donate

Med n’ Ed has many ongoing projects which require your help. Donations support our projects and help us medicate and educate all in need. Every dollar counts.

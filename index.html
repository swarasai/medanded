Our Mission

Medical and educational access to all

A nonprofit organization committed to giving proper medical and educational access to all in need. We spread awareness about hygiene, sanity, nutrition, water safety while supporting the underprivileged in healthcare aspects.

Healthcare

One of the biggest things that we work for is to give healthcare to all. Whether that is providing supplies, funding them, or working with professionals to get them treatment.

World-Wide Resources

We provide worldwide resources consisting of medical and sanitary supplies or providing for the underprivileged through book drives.

Education

We strive to bring education about healthcare, sanity, science, and more to all. It is important that people are aware about how to properly take care of themselves to avoid health problems.

What do we do?

How Med n' Ed helps

Partner Up

Partner with doctors, medical professionals, and organizations with similar missions to give medical support to those who need it.

Fund

Fund or sponsor government hospitals or organizations that work to give medical access to people in need but lack funding.

Connect

Connect with doctors in various parts of the world to help people through checkups, prescriptions, and healthcare support.

Education

Work with underprivileged kids who lack basic educational and sanitary resources and teach healthcare, sanity, water safety, nutrition, and more.

Fundraisers

Hold engaging fundraisers that educate the crowd while collecting funds for current projects.

Events

Spread awareness through webinars, seminars, and other engaging events.

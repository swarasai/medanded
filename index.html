Our Mission

Medical and educational access to all

Med n’ Ed is a nonprofit organization committed to giving proper medical and educational access to all in need. We spread awareness about hygiene, sanity, nutrition, and water safety while supporting the underprivileged in healthcare aspects.

Healthcare

One of the biggest things that we work for is to give healthcare to all. Whether that is providing supplies, funding them, or working with professionals to get them treatment.

World-Wide Resources

We provide worldwide resources consisting of medical and sanitary supplies or providing for the underprivileged through book drives.

Education

We strive to bring education about healthcare, sanity, science, and more to all. It is important that people are aware about how to properly take care of themselves to avoid health problems.

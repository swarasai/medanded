Bake Sale

Med n’ Ed held a bake sale on April 24th in order to raise money for a children’s hospital. Through selling cookies, brownies, cupcakes, and lemon bars the officers were able to raise over 600 dollars! Thank you to everyone who came out and generously supported us. We appreciate your help and your support towards a good cause!!

Our Resource Team Presents: Yearly Wrap-Up

Over the course of the year, there has been a rise in digital volunteer opportunities and COVID-approved in-person activities offered to high school students interested in pursuing medicine. Med n’ Ed has worked to track trends regarding such extracurriculars. Although there isn’t a guarantee for these offerings to continue into future years, we encourage you to visit the organizations’ sites for current information.

American Red Cross — Blood Drive Volunteer

Provide customer service to donors and help create an enhanced donor experience. In-person; eligibility varies by location.

Destiny Hospice — Hospice Volunteer

Visit patients in their homes and offer companionship and support while caregivers get a break. In-person.

Bridge Hospice Central Coast — Hospice Volunteer

Support hospice patients through reading, music, games, or walks. In-person.

Stanford Medical Youth Science Program

A medical/science enrichment program for eligible high-school students. Check Stanford’s current program page for updated eligibility and format.

Rady Children’s Hospital Summer Medical Academy

A summer healthcare learning opportunity for teens. Check the hospital’s current site for application details.

National Geographic Kids

Educational content about animals, science, history, geography, competitions, and games.

MY HERO

Digital content-production opportunities involving inspiring stories, art, and films.

Important: resource eligibility, dates, phone numbers, and application details change. Verify each external program before republishing old details.

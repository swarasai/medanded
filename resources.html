Our Resource Team Presents To You: Yearly Wrap-Up

Over the course of this year, there has been a rise within the variety of digital volunteer opportunities and COVID-approved in-person activities offered to high school students interested in pursuing medicine. Folks at Med N’ Ed have been working to keep track of the trends regarding such extracurriculars. Although there isn’t a guarantee for these offerings to continue into next year, we encourage you to visit their sites in case they end up putting out applications for the fall. For all of our creative leaders, we hope you use this information as a source of inspiration for any events you may be interested in introducing or making yourself in communities of your own, such as the health-care related clubs at your school. Thank you!

PS – Use the ScannerTranslator app to read our resources in any language.

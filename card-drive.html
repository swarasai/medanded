Card Drive Pt. 1

Representatives from Med n’ Ed — Sadhika Mulagari and Roshni Danda — delivered over 80 cards to a senior center near them. During the pandemic, seniors were especially isolated, and Med n’ Ed wanted to help by delivering motivating cards. The cards were sent to Silver Creek Housing Center and Sunrise Villa.

Card Drive Pt. 2

Representatives from Med n’ Ed — Sadhika Mulagari, Roshni Danda, and Sudiksha Das — delivered cards to healthcare workers at Good Samaritan Hospital.

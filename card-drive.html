Card Drive Pt. 1

Representatives from Med n’ Ed – Sadhika Mulagari and Roshni Danda – delivered over 80 cards to senior center near them. We realized that during the pandemic, especially seniors have been feeling lonely as they are more isolated than usual. We wanted to help that by delivering motivating cards to them! We sent the cards to Silver Creek Housing Center, Sunrise Villa!

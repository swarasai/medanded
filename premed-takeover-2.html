Premed Takeover #2
Ingrid, a pre-med student from Miami, did an Instagram takeover from Med n’ Ed’s account. She shared what a day in her life looks like as a pre-med student, talked about time management, showed some of her classes and extracurriculars, and invited followers to ask questions about the pre-med journey.
View takeover on Instagram Ingrid’s Instagram

Highlights from the takeover:

Who is featured in this takeover?

Ingrid introduces herself as a pre-med student from Miami. She shares that she is double majoring in biology and STEM education and minoring in chemistry. She also explains that the takeover will walk followers through her day while sharing tips and time-management skills along the way.

What kinds of classes and activities does she share?

The takeover includes snapshots from her day, including a class in perspective in science and math education and an update from biochemistry. She mentions that some days involve back-to-back classes until nighttime.

What extracurricular involvement does she mention?

Ingrid shares that she is vice president of AED at FIU, the national pre-health honor society, and secretary of FIU HEAL, another pre-health club. She also notes that her extracurricular work includes sending emails, organizing sign-up sheets, and creating flyers.

What questions does she invite followers to ask?

Followers are invited to ask her questions, including what extracurriculars helped her get to where she is now and other topics related to the pre-med path.

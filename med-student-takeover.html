Med Student Takeover

Sarah, a med student that graduted from UC Berkeley did an instagram takeover from Med n’ Ed’s instagram account. This takeover’s main purpose was for our audience/followers to be able to ask questions on various different topics such as the application process, what it is like to be a med student, pros and cons, tips and more.

Instagram TakeoverSarah’s Instagram

Some questions from the takeover:

Here is a little bit about Sarah: she is a first-year medical student, Korean-American, from California, an avid dog lover, a huge foodie.

Did you take any MCAT prep courses?

Sarah used Princeton Review, while noting that many friends successfully self-studied.

How difficult did you find the MCAT?

She described the MCAT as one of the hardest parts of applying to medical school, while encouraging students not to be discouraged.

What extracurriculars did you do to get into med school?

She highlighted volunteering, clinical experiences, research and leadership.

Any general advice?

Do your best, recover from setbacks, make time for self-care, take classes you genuinely enjoy, and explore opportunities.

Premed Takeover

Courtney, a premed student from UC Berkeley did an instagram takeover from Med n’ Ed’s instagram account. This takeover’s main purpose was for our audience/followers to be able to ask questions on various different things about the application process, what it is like to be a premed student, what are some pros and cons, etc.

View takeover on InstagramCourtney’s Instagram

Here are some of the questions answered:

What are the typical classes for a premed student?

This depends on the student and major. Premed students do not have to major in biology, but they do need to complete medical-school prerequisites such as chemistry, physics, biology and biochemistry.

How do you stop procrastinating/how do you manage your time well?

Courtney emphasized getting enough sleep, using Google Calendar, assigning time blocks throughout the day, and checking tasks regularly to stay balanced.

What extracurriculars are good for pre med?

She discussed clinical work, volunteering, service and other experiences, while emphasizing that students should pursue activities they genuinely care about and tell their story authentically.

Can you explain your daily life as a premed student?

She described balancing classes, volunteering, community service and work, with a strong focus on scheduling and healthy routines.

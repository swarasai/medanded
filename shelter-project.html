Shelter Project

Partnered with Family Supportive Housing

Lesson 1

Med n’ Ed has partnered up with Family Supportive Housing to host educational workshops. In our first workshop, we taught the kids at the shelter about nutrition, exercise, food groups, and overall how to lead a healthy lifestyle.

We made the lesson as entertaining as possible to keep the kids engaged; we played games with them, including Kahoot and a board game, and had them watch a fun Arthur video. There were a total of eight kids, and we really enjoyed teaching them about maintaining a healthy lifestyle.

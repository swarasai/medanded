Premed Takeover #3

Discover the life of a premed student and answers to some frequently asked questions. Natalie, a second-year bio-chem major shares her classes, extracurriculars, and more about the life of a premed student.

Takeover Transcript:

Natalie introduced herself as a second-year bio-chem major and walked viewers through her day, classes, extracurriculars and life outside class.

What are some things you did in high school to get into the premed category?

She explained that she became much more focused late in high school and used college as an opportunity to explore medicine through honor societies, research, student groups and a podcast.

Study tips?

She said she personally works well under pressure and for long study periods, but recommended trying methods such as Pomodoro and removing distractions like a phone.

Extracurriculars

She discussed research, community-service leadership in a pre-health honor society, tutoring and working as a chemistry learning assistant.
